Companion apps for Statistics Surveyors and Geomatics Professionals Should Know — works online and offline in any browser

Descriptive statistics

Chapters 3 and 4. Paste readings, one per line or separated by commas or spaces.

The outlier score z for each reading is computed against the mean and standard deviation of the other readings, so a blunder cannot hide itself (Section 4.6).

Uncertainty propagation

Chapters 5 and 6.

One row per input: name, sensitivity, standard deviation. Contribution = sensitivity × σ. Use radians for angles (″ ÷ 206265).

Error ellipse

Chapter 8. Enter the covariance matrix of a point (East, North).

The standard ellipse contains only 39.3% of positions. For 95% the axes are multiplied by 2.448, not 1.96.

Hypothesis tests

Chapters 9 and 11. Each tab gives the verdict in plain words.

Trend fit

Chapter 12. Two columns: time and value (for example days and settlement in mm).

Look at the residual plot. A curve or U-shape means a straight line is the wrong model.

Accuracy assessment

Chapter 13. Paste checkpoint differences (dataset minus checkpoint), one per line.
