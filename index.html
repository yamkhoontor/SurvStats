Statistics Surveyors and Geomatics Professionals Should Know

Plain-Language Statistics with Worked Examples and Apps

Companion apps for measurement, computation, analysis and reporting

Yam Khoon Tor, PhD, HFSISV

These are the companion apps of the book. They run in any web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, with nothing to install. Each opens with the book’s data already entered: repeat any worked example, then change the numbers to see how the results respond. Choose an app below. For use without an internet connection, download the offline version (one zip file for Windows or Mac).

Part I

Part I Solver

Measurements (Chapters 1 to 4): mean, standard deviation and standard error, the weighted mean, confidence intervals with the t multiplier, Pope's tau test for outliers, and normal-distribution multipliers in one, two and three dimensions.

 Part II

Part II Solver

Computation (Chapters 5 to 8): EDM specifications, centring on short lines, levelling runs and tolerances, trigonometric heights, radiated points, your own uncertainty budget, adjusting a levelling loop, and error ellipses.

 Part III

Part III Solver

Analysis (Chapters 9 to 14): t, chi-square and F tests, instrument checks against specification, data snooping and MDB, the tau test, height and plan displacement tests, required precision for an Alert Level, trend fits and forecasts, hyperbolic settlement, checkpoint accuracy and control charts.

 Part IV

Part IV Solver

Reporting (Chapters 15 to 17): GUM uncertainty budgets with rectangular and triangular sources, rounding results to their uncertainty, accuracy conventions (RMSE, NSSDA, LE90, CE90), and statistical tables on demand.
All calculations run in your web browser; no data are sent anywhere.
